Privacy Policy

Clean & Green Sangran (clean&greensangran)

Updated: 9 October 2026

This policy covers the Clean & Green Sangran website and Android app, which provides access to the website, operated by the Clean & Green Sangran team. Privacy contact: chmuhammadms@gmail.com.

Information used by our services

You can browse public pages without submitting a form. Depending on the features you use, we process:

  • Membership applications: name, father's name, mobile number, address, age, occupation, interests, and optional WhatsApp number and CNIC.
  • Blood donor accounts and blood requests: contact and account information, blood group, availability, donation details, patient or attendant details, hospital address, requested units and notes.
  • Complaints and suggestions: the message and category, optional name and phone number, address, photos, and GPS coordinates if you choose to provide them.
  • Support correspondence: your email address and the information you send us.
  • Technical information: hosting and database services receive connection information such as IP address, browser information, request times and error information when serving the service.

Why information is processed

Information is used to manage membership, coordinate community work and blood requests, provide account access, respond to complaints and support requests, maintain records, and protect the service against misuse. Provide other people's information only when you have permission or another appropriate basis.

Visibility and service providers

Public community pages display selected community records, project information, financial summaries and donor information. Donor profiles selected for the public reel may be visible to visitors. Information used for coordinating blood donations may be shared with people involved in that request. Complaint references allow status lookup; keep your reference private. Uploaded complaint images use publicly accessible links: do not upload identity documents, private medical records or sensitive child-safety evidence.

The service uses Supabase for database, authentication and file storage, and Vercel for website hosting. These providers process information to operate their services. Maps and external links may connect to other providers, which receive the connection data needed to serve their content. Sending an email uses your email provider. Providers may process data outside your country. Information may also be disclosed where required by law or to address safety concerns.

Device access and local storage

Location is requested when you choose the GPS option. Images are selected by you through the device file picker. You can decline permissions and leave optional fields empty. Browser storage and caches support sessions, preferences and application data; clearing them can sign you out. The reviewed app code does not integrate an advertising SDK.

Retention, correction and deletion

Records are retained while needed for the community service, account administration, handling requests, or applicable recordkeeping and safety obligations. To request access, correction, deletion of submitted data, or deletion of your account and associated data, email chmuhammadms@gmail.com with the service used and the relevant account email or reference. Do not send your password. We may need to verify your identity. Records required for legal, financial or safety purposes may need to be retained; backup copies may persist until replaced.

Security and children

The website uses HTTPS. No online service can guarantee absolute security. Do not put sensitive personal information in public content. Parents or guardians can contact us about a child's information. Child sexual abuse and exploitation are prohibited; see our Child Safety Standards.

Changes and contact

Changes will be published on this page with an updated date. Contact the Clean & Green Sangran team at chmuhammadms@gmail.com for questions.
