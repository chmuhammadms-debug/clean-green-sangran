Child Safety Standards

Clean & Green Sangran (clean&greensangran)

Updated: 9 October 2026

These standards apply to all users, submissions and content in Clean & Green Sangran, including its Android app and website.

Zero tolerance for child abuse and exploitation

Child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), including child sexual abuse material (CSAM), is prohibited. This includes grooming, sexual solicitation or exploitation of minors, sexual extortion, trafficking, and creating, requesting, distributing or linking to abusive material involving children. A child is anyone under 18.

Report a concern

Open Report a child safety concern from the app's Safety & Privacy links. Contact the team at chmuhammadms@gmail.com. Give the relevant page, account or reference and a short description. Do not download, attach or forward suspected CSAM. Do not post child-safety reports or sensitive evidence in public complaint photos.

Response and enforcement

Our safety procedure is to review reports, restrict or remove violating content and take appropriate action against offending accounts. Confirmed CSAM must be reported to the relevant regional or national authority in accordance with applicable law. Relevant information should be preserved and disclosed only as lawfully required. These standards require the team to cooperate with lawful investigations and follow applicable child-safety laws.

Safety contact

The Clean & Green Sangran team can be contacted about CSAE prevention, enforcement and compliance at chmuhammadms@gmail.com.

If a child is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services or the appropriate child-protection authority. This app is not an emergency service.
